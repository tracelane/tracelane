<Note>
**Ingest is ONE meter — a gateway call and a raw OTel span both count the
same way.** Whichever path sends data (the gateway's
`/v1/chat/completions` proxy, or an OTel exporter pointed at Tracelane), what
you're billed on is bytes accepted, not a trace count or a separate span
limit.
</Note>

<Warning>
**Size by ingest bytes, not by run count.** If you send OpenTelemetry spans
directly (the [framework integrations](/integrations/langchain)), one agent
run is many spans, and each span is its own share of ingest bytes.

Measured on 2026-08-31 with the standard community instrumentors:

| One run of… | Spans |
|---|---|
| a LangChain `prompt \| model \| parser` chain | **4** |
| a two-node LangGraph agent | **11** |
| a LlamaIndex `llm.complete()` | **1** |
| a two-step Vercel AI tool-calling run | **6** |

A busier framework that retries tasks sends more spans, and more spans is
more ingest bytes. If you're unsure, run your agent once against a free
account and check the ingest total on your usage page before committing to a
plan.
</Warning>

<Note>
**Retention has two windows.** The **indexed window** is how far back queries
run fast: Free 3 days, Builder 30 days, Team 90 days, Business 180 days,
Enterprise 365+ days. **Queryable history** is how far back your data stays
queryable at all, from cold storage: Free 30 days, every paid tier 2 years
(7 years on Enterprise). Retention is not customer-configurable — there is no
retention setting in the product.
</Note>

<Note>
**Uptime.** Tracelane does not offer a contractual uptime SLA or service credits.
The [SLO](/slo) dashboard tracks error-budget burn against a default availability
target, and burn-rate alerts are available on paid plans.
</Note>

## How usage is metered

Six meters, all live on every paid tier — Builder included. Usage above your
included allowance bills continuously per unit; there is no rollover and no
hard-cap.

| Meter | Rate | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Ingest | $0.20 / GB accepted | Bytes accepted through the gateway or via OTLP — one meter, both paths. |
| Hot indexed window | $16.00/GB-month (0–500) · $8.00 (500–2,000) · $4.50 (2,000–10,000) · $3.00 (10,000+) | Resident GB-month inside your plan's indexed window — priced as **query capacity**, not disk. |
| Series | $0.008 / series-month | Distinct metric series (provider/model/attribute combinations) tracked this month. |
| Query compute | $0.15 / scan-unit | ClickHouse bytes scanned to answer your queries; 1 scan-unit = 1 GB scanned. |
| Cold archive | $0.08 / GB-month | Resident GB-month once data ages past the indexed window, while still inside queryable history — measured as the mean daily cold GB over the month, the same basis as the hot window. Every paid tier includes 24× its monthly ingest allowance in cold (two years of included ingest); the meter bills only beyond that. History you import counts toward cold from the day it lands. |
| Evaluations | $0.005 / judge run | Each LLM-judge run against your traces or datasets. |

<Note>
**Resident GB is the logical size of what you sent, averaged daily across the
month — not peak, not compressed bytes on disk. The hot window is priced as
query capacity, not disk.**
</Note>

<Note>
**Never metered, and why:** per host or peak host · per seat on any paid tier
· a separate egress line (egress is priced into ingest and query) · retention
as a multiplier · OTel attributes reclassified as premium metrics.
</Note>

**No rollover.** Unused allowance does not carry to next month.

**Incident burst.** Any single day up to 5× your trailing 30-day average is
billed at included rates, not overage.

**Warnings at 75% and 90%** of any meter, with a month-end projection and a
"what's using your window" breakdown by project, service, capture state and
trace shape.

**Spend ceiling — off by default.** Set a monthly ceiling if you want one. At
the ceiling, AUTO-AGE switches on: the oldest days leave your indexed window
early and stay queryable in cold storage. Ingest keeps running and nothing is
lost.

<Note>
**Ingest is never blocked by billing state, on any tier.** A recorder that
stops recording because of an invoice is not a recorder.
</Note>

## Free

One workspace per account, reclaimed after 14 idle days, no card required, no
overage — Free ages out rather than returning 429s.

## Policies

- **Dunning.** Failed payments retry at days 1, 5 and 14, then the workspace
  drops to Free. Data is held 30 days.
- **Refunds.** 30 days on the base subscription, first billing cycle only.
  Usage charges are non-refundable.
- **Enterprise onboarding.** A one-time $5,000 fee covers migration and a
  named engineer for 30 days.
- **Prepaid credits.** $5,000 buys $5,750 of usable credit; $20,000 buys
  $24,000. Credits expire after 12 months.

<Note>
**Audit ledger.** The tamper-evident ledger, hash chain, Rekor anchoring and
self-verification are included on every tier. Seven-year ledger retention is
included on Enterprise.
</Note>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="What happens if I go over my included allowance?">
You get a warning at 75% and 90% of any meter, with a month-end projection. Usage above
your included allowance bills continuously per unit — there is no hard-cap and no 429.
Any single day up to 5× your trailing 30-day average bills at included rates (incident
burst), so a one-day spike doesn't turn into overage. An optional spend ceiling (off by
default) switches on AUTO-AGE at the limit you set: the oldest days leave your indexed
window early and stay queryable in cold storage, and ingest keeps running. No surprise
bills.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How many people can use one workspace?">
Team management is live. Seats are **unlimited on every paid tier** — Builder, Team,
Business and Enterprise all invite without limit. Free is single-seat. Invite by email
from **Settings → Team** — invitations are sent through WorkOS, and each invitee is given
a `member` or `viewer` role (owner is a separate explicit grant). On Free, a second invite
is refused with a typed `seat_limit_reached` error. Provider keys are BYOK and
workspace-scoped, so every seat shares the workspace's keys.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What inline guardrails run on every request?">
Heuristic guardrails run inline at the gateway on every request. **Every
agent-safety rail is free** — on the hosted free tier and in an Apache-2.0
self-host alike:

| Free on every deployment | What it catches |
| --- | --- |
| MCP rug-pull detection | a tool whose definition changes after you approved it |
| Lethal-trifecta detection | untrusted content, private-data access and an exfiltration-capable tool converging in one request |
| Prompt-injection patterns | injection in prompts, tool descriptions and results |
| Tool-schema validation | malformed or unexpected tool calls |
| Cost ceiling | a request that would exceed the configured spend limit |

Team and above add the data-governance and quality rails: secrets/PII detection
and redaction, output-format policy, system-prompt-leak detection, and
topic/competitor policy.

**How rug-pull detection works in practice:** it compares each request's tool
definition against the one you approved. Tracelane records every tool definition
it sees on your traffic, so approving is one click in **Guardrails → Tool
pinning** — there is nothing to configure and no tool JSON to hand-author. After
that, any change to the tool's name, schema or description is flagged as
definition drift on the next request. Tracelane stores the definition *hash*,
never the tool text. Approving pins a definition; it never grants a tool any
capability, which is a separate owner-only setting.

Each rail can flag or halt the request at the gateway, inline, per the
configured policy — detection is observe-first by default, because a
false-positive block is worse than the failure it prevents.

Browser stuck-loop prediction, A2A/A2UI catalog conformance and argument-drift
detection are on the roadmap: the detectors exist, but they gate on payload
fields (`mcp_server_name`, `tool_name`, `protocol`) that a
`/v1/chat/completions` request does not carry, so they do not fire on LLM
traffic today.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How does Tracelane compare to LangSmith Engine (launched May 13, 2026)?">
Engine clusters production failures after they happen, then drafts PRs and online evaluators in your LangSmith UI. Tracelane sits one step earlier in the agent lifecycle: inline heuristic guardrails at the gateway, tamper-evident promotion record on every deploy, and EWMA drift-based rollback on metrics you post to the observe endpoint (cost, latency, guardrail-fire rate — see [prompt promotion](/prompt-promotion) for what is autonomous and what is not). Engine requires the LangSmith trace surface + LangChain-managed inference keys per LangSmith AI ToS. Tracelane is OTel GenAI semconv-native, BYOK zero markup, Apache 2.0 full stack, self-hostable. See [vs LangSmith Engine](/comparisons/vs-langsmith-engine).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What's included in the audit ledger?">
A tamper-evident record of what your agents actually did. Every gateway-proxied request appends a hash-chained audit entry. Entries are batched (100 by default), each batch gets an RFC 6962 Merkle root, and that root is signed with your workspace's own Ed25519 key — the Merkle root is per batch, not per span. Anchored batches carry a resolved Sigstore Rekor v2 inclusion proof on a best-effort basis, with universal per-run anchoring on the roadmap. **All of this — the hash chain, Ed25519 signing, Rekor anchoring and offline self-verification — is included on every tier**, not sold as a separate add-on.

**Scope, stated plainly:** only calls proxied through the Tracelane gateway are chained. Spans sent straight from an SDK or an OTLP exporter are stored and queryable, but they are not part of the chain.

Self-verify with `tlane verify --tenant-pubkey` on your own export — no Tracelane involvement, no trust in us required. The bulk regulatory export (`/v1/audit/export`, the Article-12-style evidence package) and 7-year ledger retention are **Enterprise** capabilities. See [Audit Ledger](/audit-ledger).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a free trial for paid tiers?">
Free hosted (one workspace, reclaimed after 14 idle days, no card required) is permanently free for non-commercial use. There is no paid-tier free trial — the free hosted tier and OSS self-host are the trial.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I self-host?">
Yes. Apache 2.0 full stack on GitHub (`github.com/tracelane/tracelane`). Self-host the full stack with a single-node Docker Compose (ClickHouse-backed) — no managed cloud required. Enterprise gets a commercial-support license. See [Self-hosting](/self-hosting).
</Accordion>
