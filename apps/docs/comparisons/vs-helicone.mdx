Helicone was reported to have been acquired by Mintlify in early 2026 (per press coverage; treat the specifics as unconfirmed).

Tracelane is Apache 2.0 across the entire stack — gateway, ingest, SDKs and self-host.
If licensing matters to your review, read each project's `LICENSE` file and take your own
counsel's view: Tracelane's is [Apache-2.0](https://github.com/tracelane/tracelane/blob/main/LICENSE),
and Helicone's `ai-gateway` carries a
[GPL-3.0 `LICENSE`](https://github.com/Helicone/ai-gateway/blob/main/LICENSE).

## Migration

```bash
tlane migrate helicone --apply
```

This scans your `.env*` files and SDK call sites, then rewrites the Helicone base URL and
auth headers (`Helicone-Auth`) to their Tracelane equivalents — a config + environment
migration, shown as a diff before writing. It does **not** import historical trace data.
Most teams cut over the same afternoon; new traces flow from the moment you redeploy.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Does Tracelane copy any Helicone code?">
No. Tracelane's gateway is original work under Apache 2.0, and we take no Helicone package as a dependency.

You do not have to take that on trust. Our `deny.toml` sets a license allowlist for the whole Rust dependency tree, and GPL/AGPL are denied by omission from it — a copyleft crate cannot enter the build without the `cargo-deny (bans + licenses + advisories)` CI job failing. Every third-party dependency and its license is also enumerated in the CycloneDX SBOM published with each release, so you can audit the tree yourself.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is Tracelane feature-equivalent to Helicone?">
Tracelane covers 30+ providers, BYOK with envelope encryption, usage/token tracking, OTel GenAI semconv, and a full Apache 2.0 self-host. On top of that it ships a tamper-evident audit ledger you can verify offline, inline heuristic guardrails, and a Prompt Promotion workflow. Compare that surface against your current tool's own documentation — feature parity is a moving target and we would rather you check than trust our summary of someone else's product.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to my historical Helicone data?">
Your historical traces stay where they are — `tlane migrate helicone` is a config + environment migration, not a trace-data importer, so it does not pull your past Helicone traces across. New traces flow into Tracelane from the moment you redeploy. A historical-trace importer (Helicone JSON dump → OTel GenAI semconv) is on the roadmap; until it ships, export from Helicone for archival and start fresh in Tracelane.
</Accordion>
