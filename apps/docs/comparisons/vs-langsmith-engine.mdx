Teams evaluating Tracelane often already run LangSmith. Rather than summarise someone
else's product — which we cannot keep accurate and you should not have to trust — this
page states plainly what Tracelane ships and what it does not, so you can compare it
against whatever you run today.

For LangSmith's current capabilities, pricing and terms, read
[LangChain's own documentation](https://docs.langchain.com/langsmith).

## What Tracelane is

Tracelane is a **flight recorder for AI agents**: full-fidelity capture of every model
call, plus a tamper-evident ledger that a third party can verify offline, without
access to our systems and without trusting us.

## What Tracelane ships

### A tamper-evident audit ledger

Every audit event lands in a Merkle-batched hash chain. Each tenant gets its own Ed25519
signing key, and the chain is signed per tenant.

The property that matters: **you can verify it without us.** Export the ledger and run
`tlane verify --tenant-pubkey <your key>` on your own machine. The verification is
offline and independent — if a record had been altered or removed, the chain check fails.
Anchored batches additionally carry a resolved Sigstore Rekor v2 inclusion proof on a
best-effort basis; universal per-run anchoring is on the roadmap.

See [Audit Ledger](/audit-ledger) for the format and the verification procedure.

### Full-fidelity capture, on a neutral wire format

Traces are emitted as OTel GenAI semantic conventions — an open standard, not a
Tracelane-specific envelope. Capture defaults to full fidelity rather than sampled, so
the recorder does not silently drop the trace you later need.

### Observe-first guardrails

Guardrails run inline at the gateway and are **deliberately observe-first**: they detect
and record rather than block by default. This is a considered position, not a missing
feature — for most teams a false-positive block is worse than the failure it would have
prevented. Detection today is heuristic and deterministic; a multi-model ML ensemble is
on the roadmap and is not shipped.

### BYOK at 0% markup

Bring your own provider keys across 30+ providers. Keys are stored with envelope
encryption, with the AAD bound to `(tenant_id, provider_id)`. We add no markup to your
provider spend.

### Auto-rollback on objective metrics

Prompt promotions can roll back automatically on EWMA thresholds over cost, latency,
error rate and guardrail-fire rate — objective signals, not a human noticing a
regression days later.

### Apache 2.0, self-hostable

The entire stack is Apache 2.0 and runs self-hosted on single-node Docker Compose. Paid
plans start at $59/mo (Builder); self-hosting the open-source stack is free.

## What Tracelane does not do

- **Failure clustering.** Not shipped. Clustering-adjacent capabilities are on the V1.5
  roadmap.
- **Automated PR drafting against your repo.** Not shipped, and not on the near roadmap.
- **Blocking enforcement by default.** Deliberate — see observe-first above.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Does Tracelane do failure clustering today?">
No. Tracelane positions on full-fidelity capture and the tamper-evident record, not on
post-failure clustering. Clustering-adjacent capabilities are on the V1.5 roadmap. If
clustering is your primary need today, Tracelane is not yet the tool for that job.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I migrate from LangSmith to Tracelane?">
Not with a one-command importer — that is on the roadmap. `tlane migrate helicone` and
`tlane import-litellm` ship today; a LangSmith equivalent does not.

Because the wire format is OTel GenAI semconv, the practical path is to instrument once
and dual-write during cutover, then cut the old destination when you are satisfied.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I run Tracelane alongside what I already have?">
Yes. Tracelane sits at the gateway and emits OTel GenAI semconv, so it can run in
parallel with an existing tracing destination during evaluation. Nothing requires you to
cut over before you have compared them on your own traffic.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What does 'tamper-evident promotion record' mean?">
Every promotion event (staging → production) is recorded in a tamper-evident
Merkle-batched hash chain with Ed25519 per-tenant signing. Anchored batches carry a
resolved Sigstore Rekor v2 inclusion proof on a best-effort basis; universal per-run
anchoring is on the roadmap.

"Tamper-evident" means alteration is *detectable*, not that it is prevented. The check is
independently reproducible with `tlane verify --tenant-pubkey` against the exported
ledger. See [Audit Ledger](/audit-ledger).
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
